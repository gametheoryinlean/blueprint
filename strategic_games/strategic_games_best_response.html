Best Response

A strategy $\sigma_i$ is a best response for player $i$ to the profile $\sigma$ if no unilateral deviation can improve $i$'s payoff:

$$\forall s'_i \in S_i, \quad u_i(\sigma[i \mapsto s'_i]) \le u_i(\sigma).$$
