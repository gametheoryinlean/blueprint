Payoff Negation in Zero-Sum Games

If $G$ is zero-sum, then at every profile $\sigma$ the payoff of either player determines the other:

$$u_1(\sigma) = -u_0(\sigma), \qquad u_0(\sigma) = -u_1(\sigma).$$

This is the algebraic reduction that lets later value and optimality statements track only the row player's payoff.
