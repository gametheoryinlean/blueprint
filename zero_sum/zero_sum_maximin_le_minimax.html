Maximin is Bounded by Minimax

For every finite matrix game,

$$\max_x \min_j E_A(x,j) \le \min_y \max_i E_A(i,y).$$

This is the weak-duality inequality: player I's best guaranteed payoff cannot exceed player II's best upper bound on player I's payoff.
