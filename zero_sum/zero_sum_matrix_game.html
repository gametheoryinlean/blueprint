Matrix Game

A finite two-player zero-sum matrix game is specified by finite row and column sets $I,J$ and a payoff matrix

$$A : I \times J \to \mathbb{R}.$$

Player I chooses a row, player II chooses a column, player I receives $A(i,j)$, and player II receives $-A(i,j)$.
