Maximin and Minimax Values

For a mixed row strategy $x$, player I's guarantee is the worst expected payoff against a pure column:

$$\gamma_I(x) = \min_{j \in J} E_A(x,j).$$

For a mixed column strategy $y$, player II's guarantee against player I is the largest payoff player I can obtain from a pure row:

$$\gamma_{II}(y) = \max_{i \in I} E_A(i,y).$$

The maximin value is \(\sup_x \gamma_I(x)\), and the minimax value is \(\inf_y \gamma_{II}(y)\).
