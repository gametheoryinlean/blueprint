Nash Payoff Uniqueness in Zero-Sum Games

In a zero-sum game, any two Nash equilibria give the same payoff to player 0 and the same payoff to player 1. Thus the equilibrium payoff is a value of the game, independent of which Nash equilibrium is chosen.

The proof compares two equilibria through cross profiles. One player's best response inequality is converted into the other player's inequality using zero-sum payoff negation.
