Dantzig Proof Spine for Minimax

Dantzig's proof represents the minimax theorem through an augmented linear system. A basis state stores rows of the inverse basis matrix, a lexicographic condition prevents cycling, and optimality inequalities encode the game-theoretic guarantees.

At an optimal basis, the extracted row strategy is nonnegative and satisfies the row player's guarantee inequalities. The same basis data extracts a column strategy for player II. The pivot step updates the inverse basis while preserving the lexicographic condition and strictly decreasing the leading row in lexicographic order.
