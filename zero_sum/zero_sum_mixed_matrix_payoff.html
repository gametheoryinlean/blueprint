Expected Payoff of a Matrix Game

For mixed strategies $x \in \Delta(I)$ and $y \in \Delta(J)$, the expected payoff of the matrix game $A$ is

$$E_A(x,y) = \sum_{i \in I}\sum_{j \in J} x_i y_j A(i,j).$$

The one-sided forms $E_A(x,j)$ and $E_A(i,y)$ describe mixed play against a pure response.
